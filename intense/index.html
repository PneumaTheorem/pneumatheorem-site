Physical goods

Intense

Everyday carry objects in metal and leather, designed from first principles. The design language is shared with LADE.

Prototyping

INTENSE is PneumaTheorem's physical craft label. It is separate because the logistics and the product type are different; the design language is the same as the rest of the studio. We make things meant to be carried every day: clear in structure, clean to the touch, and built to be used.

What we care about

Crafted in Taiwan with in-house studio quality control. The emphasis is on tactile finish, structural durability, and clean daily usability.

Where we are

Prototyping and material testing are under way. News of the first piece will be announced first on our official news board and social channels.
