Installing LADE Aquarium

Early-access builds are not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm the first launch. It takes a minute, and you only do it once.

Why the warning? Notarization requires an Apple Developer membership — the first LADE milestone. Once it is in place, you will not need this page.

macOS 15 Sequoia and later

  1. Move LADE Aquarium to Applications

    Unzip the download and drag LADE Aquarium into your Applications folder.

  2. Open it once

    macOS says it cannot verify the developer. Click Done — not Move to Trash.

  3. Allow it in System Settings

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to LADE Aquarium. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

  4. Open LADE Aquarium again

    Click Open in the dialog that follows. From now on, LADE Aquarium opens normally.

macOS 13 Ventura or 14 Sonoma

Control-click LADE Aquarium in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again in the dialog. The System Settings route above works too.

Permissions LADE Aquarium may ask for

  • Calendars — To show your next event on the desktop. Events are read on your Mac only.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording — So the music ring and her singing can follow what you play. Analysed in real time only — nothing is recorded, stored or sent.
  • Accessibility — Asked only if you turn on “Replace the System Volume Popup”; used only to receive the volume, mute and brightness keys.

All are optional; LADE Aquarium works without them.

Uninstalling

Quit LADE Aquarium, move it to the Trash, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Aquarium to remove its settings.
