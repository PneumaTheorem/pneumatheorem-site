What it does

A desktop that is never quite the same twice.

LADE is the name of the series — its game is still to come. Aquarium is its desktop: your screen becomes the aquarium she lives in.

01

Light that follows your day

Hand-painted light for night, morning, noon and dusk, blended as the hours pass — through the seasons and the 24 solar terms.

02

Your weather, on her

Optionally follows the real weather where you are: rain beads and runs down the glass, snow falls, frost creeps in, stars come out.

03

Below the surface

Take the scene underwater. Caustics ripple across her, shafts of light reach down, and the colours deepen as you go.

04

She sings along

When you play music, she sings along — her mouth moves with the lyrics that are playing, and a ring of light follows the sound. Audio is analysed on your Mac only — nothing is recorded, stored or sent.

05

Time at a glance

Clock, date, forecast and your next calendar event, drawn in a quiet dot-matrix style on the desktop itself.

06

Out of your way

When a window covers the desktop, she pauses. No accounts, no analytics, no ads.

07

A drawer in the notch

Move the pointer to the notch and a drawer opens: switch the time of day, weather, scene, character and quality in one click, with what's playing right there.

08

Work Mode

One click packs the wallpaper away and frees all the memory and GPU it was using; the drawer keeps working. Click again to bring it back (it takes a few seconds to reload).

09

Cursors to match

Swap the system-wide mouse cursor for a set that matches her style, or keep the system default. Greyed out on macOS versions that don't allow it.

Why we made it

One terminal for the services you use.

A desktop should be pleasant to look at — and it is also the one place you see all day. LADE is built as a terminal for your services: the clock, weather, calendar, focus timer, reminders, ledger and music in one place, signed in with the accounts you already have — Google, or each service’s own.

Many single-purpose apps charge for one small thing that AI and the platforms are quickly making ordinary. LADE replaces that pile of apps with one quiet companion; you no longer open a dozen windows for a dozen chores.

Every widget has a price — many are $0 — and a subscription unlocks all of them at once, including what comes next. Characters are separate content packs. Our aim is one integrated service around your day; Aquarium is the first piece.

Hand-painted light

Same scene, a different hour.

Each part of the day has its own lighting, painted for the character rather than filtered on top — her hair, skin and dress each take their own light.

Stills assembled from LADE’s own layers. In motion she also breathes, her hair sways, and the weather plays across the scene.

Scenes

One season, one room, one place outside.

Each scene has its own day, night and underwater version. Scenes are free in the app, and more are added over time.

Questions

Why does macOS warn me when I first open LADE Aquarium?

Early-access builds are not yet notarized by Apple — notarization is the first milestone. Until then, macOS asks you to confirm once. Here is how.

Does LADE collect my data?

No analytics, no ads. Features that connect to a service — your calendar, the weather, lyrics — talk to that service directly from your Mac, with its own account where one is needed. The privacy page lists each one.

Will it slow my Mac down?

LADE pauses when a window covers the desktop, so it draws only while you can see it. On a laptop that stays on all day, choose Standard quality or 30 fps on the Performance page.

Which quality should I pick?

The Performance page lines the qualities up for your screen: HD matches your screen (the default), Enhanced is finer and runs warmer, Standard saves power. PRO renders the lighting effects at full resolution too, and frame rate can be 30 or 60.

Can I stream or record LADE?

Yes — including on monetized channels. See Streaming & videos.

Is there a Windows version?

A Windows port is in progress. There is no date yet — progress will be posted in our news and on X.

Which outfits are included?

The Maid outfit, included free with the app. The Valkyrie Pack is a paid add-on; the Aristocrat outfit becomes free for everyone at 300 copies.
