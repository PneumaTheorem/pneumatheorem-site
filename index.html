Studio

One person, one world.

PneumaTheorem, Inc. is a Delaware company run from Taiwan by its founder, Kevin Tao. The renderer, the tools and the character pipeline behind LADE are made here.

LADE pays for its own road: each sales milestone funds the next step — notarization, free outfits, Steam and Windows, widgets chosen by supporters, and a second character.

Press, collaboration or anything else: kevin@pneumatheorem.org
