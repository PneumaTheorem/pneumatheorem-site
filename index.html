Our first release · Early access

LADE Aquarium

A living wallpaper for macOS. Solmete lives on your desktop: the light follows the hours of your day, real weather can appear on the glass, and the scene can sink under water. When you play music, she sings along — animated to the music. When a window covers the desktop, she pauses.

  • PlatformmacOS 13 or later (Windows port in progress)
  • StatusEarly access
  • Priceitch.io US$4.99 · BOOTH ¥750
  • LanguagesEnglish · 中文 · 日本語

What we make

Small worlds, built to run all day.

01

Characters that live with you

Hair, expressions and light are separate layers that move on their own — rendered in real time, and quiet enough to leave running.

02

Light, weather and water

Our own renderer: hand-painted light for each part of the day, rain, snow and frost on the glass, and caustics under water.

03

Games next

A game set in the same world is in early design. We will share its development, bit by bit, on YouTube and X.

About PneumaTheorem

PneumaTheorem is an independent studio with a background in trading software and physics modeling. LADE is our first consumer product line, built to be an all-in-one desktop platform. From here we are expanding into games, physics-model plug-ins and AI tools, building toward comprehensive services.
