Privacy

Effective 15 November 2026 · English is the governing version.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties: fonts and images are served from this domain. The colour-scheme and language switches store nothing. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes each request in order to deliver the page.

LADE Aquarium

LADE Aquarium runs on your Mac. There is no LADE account, no analytics, tracking or advertising, and we do not receive or sell your personal data.

Stays on your Mac

  • Calendar events — read with your permission to show your next event on the desktop.
  • Now playing — title, artist and artwork of the music you are playing.
  • System audio — analysed in real time for the music ring and her singing. Never recorded, stored or sent. macOS asks for “Screen & System Audio Recording” for this.
  • Window positions — used to step out of the way of your windows.
  • Your entries and settings — expenses, focus sessions, water and meals, coins and unlocked backgrounds, settings.
  • Volume and brightness keys — only if you turn on “Replace the System Volume Popup”. macOS then asks for Accessibility; it is used only to receive the volume, mute and brightness keys. No other input is read, and nothing is stored or sent.

Each service handles this data under its own privacy policy. Any service your Mac contacts can see your IP address.

Purchases

You buy LADE Aquarium through a store (currently BOOTH, operated by pixiv Inc.), under that store’s privacy policy. We receive what the store gives sellers to deliver the order; we never receive your payment details.

Your choices

You can turn real weather and lyrics off, and withdraw the calendar, recording and accessibility permissions in System Settings at any time. Deleting the app and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Aquarium removes everything it stored.

Children

LADE is not directed at children under 13 (or the age set by the law of your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Who is responsible
